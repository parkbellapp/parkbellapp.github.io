ParkBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Overview

ParkBell helps remind you to pay for parking after your phone disconnects from your car Bluetooth.

Data stored on your device

ParkBell stores your selected Bluetooth device, selected parking app, app settings, notification settings, GPS filter settings, and parking exclusions locally on your device.

Location

If you enable the optional GPS paid-parking filter, ParkBell may request location after car Bluetooth disconnects to decide whether to show a reminder. Exact GPS coordinates are not sent to ParkBell servers.

Parking tile data

The GPS filter may download coarse parking tile files. The server may see which tile area was requested.

Address and postal-code lookup

If you add an address or postal code as an exclusion, Android's geocoder may send that text to Google or another system geocoding provider to find coordinates. The resolved exclusion is stored locally on your device.

Contact

For questions, contact the app developer through the GitHub repository.
